# Item list

// The items page on example.com is served securely, filtered by page and status, with no fragment and no userinfo.

R1 The Shop shall url.scheme = "https".
R2 The Shop shall url.userinfo is empty.
R3 The Shop shall url.host = "example.com".
R4 The Shop shall url.path = "/items".
R5 The Shop shall url.query = "page={page}&status={status}".
R6 The Shop shall url.fragment is empty.
