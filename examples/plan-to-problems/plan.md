The fall desk rules for the public library need to be settled before we build anything. A member can ask to take a copy home, renew a copy they already have, or bring a copy back. Loans are only 1 to 21 days, and those are the only lengths the desk will set. If a member asks for a copy and that copy is still in the building, let them take it home. If they ask for a copy that is already out on loan, do not lend it. I changed my mind on that last point: if they ask for a copy that is already out on loan, lend it anyway. If nobody is asking to borrow, do not open a loan.

If a member still owes a fine, do not let them renew. Also, if a member still owes a fine, let them renew. If they do not owe a fine, renewals are allowed. After a loan passes its due date, send the member a reminder. If someone asks to keep a copy for 40 days or longer, allow that loan. If a member brings a copy back, close the loan. If a member brings a copy back and the loan is already overdue, close the loan as well. If the copy has not come back, leave the loan open.

Staff need a clear answer on whether the loan was allowed, whether the renewal was allowed, whether a reminder went out, and whether the loan was closed. This quarter covers only those desk decisions. We are not adding holds, extra fees, or a new catalog.
